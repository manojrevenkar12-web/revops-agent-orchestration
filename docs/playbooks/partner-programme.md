# Partner programme: deal registration rules

Who uses this: Partner Managers, Deal Desk.

## Registration decisions

- Approve when there is no open direct opportunity on the account and no other partner holds an approved registration.
- Reject when the account already has an open direct opportunity in Proposal or Negotiation: the deal is already being
  worked by Sales, and the partner is told so.
- Escalate to the partner manager when the account has an early-stage direct opportunity (Discovery, Solution Design,
  Technical Validation) or another partner already holds an approved registration. The partner manager decides with the
  account executive within two business days.
- Registrations above 50,000 EUR always get a partner manager's review, even without a conflict.

## After approval

The approved registration is linked to the opportunity in Salesforce (Partner Account field), which makes the partner
eligible for the registration discount and shows the deal in partner forecasts.

## Matching the company

Match on the company's web domain first, then on the name. Names are written inconsistently (capitals, legal suffixes,
local spellings), so a name-only match is a suggestion for the partner manager, never an automatic conflict.
